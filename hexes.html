Mundi hex drill-down: six styles

Each planet hex opened as a walkable local map (seed 1, content-aware 10×). Rows are hexes, cards are styles: from above, then at eye level. The blurred ground around each tile is its neighbouring hexes. Tap a card to open it live, then drag to orbit, pinch to zoom, and switch styles from the panel. The time slider (▶ plays a whole day) moves the sun along its real path for that spot on the planet.

The planet (double-click a hex there to open any other)
